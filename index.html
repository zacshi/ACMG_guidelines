    This tool supports qualified variant scientists and clinicians. It does not replace expert review or gene-specific criteria from ClinGen Variant Curation Expert Panels. Nothing you enter leaves your browser, and nothing is stored between visits, so use Save case to keep your work.

    It implements the 2015 ACMG/AMP guideline with ClinGen SVI refinements noted on individual criteria. The draft ACMG/AMP v4 revision is not implemented.
